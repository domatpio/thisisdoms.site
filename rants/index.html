dom's angry over nothing again

an open letter to the Students for Socialist Revolution (RCA) of UMN

The SSR is a student-led Trot club at UMN, where I have the displeasure of reading all the dumb bullshit on their braindead posters and stickers.

I am here to propose a formal request: please shut the fuck up already. I am tired of seeing programmatist idiocy where ever I walk, condescending to people like you are at all smart or should at all be taken seriously.

You morons forswore serious class analysis the moment you started printing those stupid fucking "Epstein class" stickers; that anyone takes you seriously is one of the greatest mysteries of all time.

These midwit troglodytes will get done reading the Manifesto and start thinking they're God's Great Proletarian Hero(tm) and feel the need to plaster their idiotic bullshit on every single lightpost in the Twin Cities, or at least that's what it feels like anyway.

If I had the sheer masochistic tendency to talk/debate with these individuals, I would surely die afterwards; whether its environmentally-caused acute brain damage or suicide is about a 50/50 chance.

Singing the Internationale won't get "le heckin global revolutierino!!!" anywhere, you just look like a bunch of fucking dweebs, which is coincidentally the case.

Stop plastering your bourgeois-populist idiocy everywhere, and let me go about my day. If you guys weren't reserved to weird kids who still worship Lenin and were actually popular as a result of your genuinely mind-numbing rhetoric, you'd be just as annoying as the TPUSA idiots here too.

You are idiots, and you will do no revolution, fuck off and let me walk around my campus without being reminded that there are genuinely peoplpe as unintelligent as you.

okay bye now
